About

A senior creative project connecting stories, technology and community

Overview

What is Noneun Creator?

Noneun Creator is a program that connects stories, technology and community to support seniors in passing on cultural heritage and creating new work. Starting from traditional Korean folktales, we help seniors capture their experience and wisdom and share it with the world in new ways.

About

What we do

  • Sharing cultural heritage

    We help seniors retell traditional folktales in modern, engaging formats and share them.

  • Training senior storytellers

    We teach the skills to make story videos with digital tools and social media.

  • Bringing generations together

    Through partnerships with kindergartens and local groups, we create chances for seniors and children to meet and work together.

Our goal

Our goal

Our goal is to help seniors turn their life and wisdom into stories and connect those stories with the world through digital technology.

By making videos based on folktales, we shine a new light on fading cultural heritage and help seniors confidently tell their own stories at local events and in their communities. Along the way, they build practical digital skills such as using a smartphone, editing video and social media. It's more than a class: it's a journey to regain confidence and the joy of creating.

Storybook

Our senior storybook is out

Noneun Creator offers media training for older adults and widens their chances to express themselves and create online. It began as a way to help seniors share their stories and experience as content in an aging society.

The new storybook includes traditional Korean folktales such as The Old Man with a Lump, The People Who Saw a Mirror for the First Time and The Devoted Brothers. Each tale is short and easy to read aloud, the way grandparents tell stories to their grandchildren, with notes on the traditions and story structure.

Next, we'll share the content our seniors create from this book on Instagram, YouTube and other social media, and grow it into education programs with local kindergartens. Thank you for your interest and support.
